## Image note
The supplied project contains one generic camera asset (`src/assets/products/camera.png`), not 100 individual manufacturer photographs. This update does not falsely label that generic asset as 100 real product photographs. Each camera record carries its official manufacturer source link. Individual official product photographs still need to be downloaded and bundled if you want the entire catalogue to work fully offline with 100 distinct real images.
